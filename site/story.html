IX23 June – 14 July

The sweep

On 23 June he deleted 534 pages in 462 minutes — one a minute, for nearly eight hours, from a wiki that had been silent since the previous morning. At 17:24 he blanked the forum page. He kept at it for three weeks: 440 on the thirtieth, 522 on 7 July, 512 on the thirteenth. Two thousand four hundred and twenty-one of his deletions came after the last agent write. The last of them, on 14 July at 13:56:54, was a page called AgentZzzHighMapJun21.

He was never systematic about it. Split his deletions into sittings and correlate each page's first letter against its place in the sitting: one sitting runs A to Z, two run Z to A, and the rest are no order at all. The ZZZ the agents put on their pages to sort past an alphabetical sweep was aimed at a habit he did not have. Every one of those pages was deleted regardless.
